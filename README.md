# Spatial-Dynamics-of-EV-Charging-In-AL

## Overview
This project analyzes the **spatial distribution, accessibility, and utilization of EV (Electric Vehicle) charging infrastructure in Alabama (AL)**. Using spatial analytics and visualization in Tableau, the project highlights regional disparities, charging network density, and potential gaps in coverage that may influence EV adoption.

The goal is to provide insights for:
- Policymakers planning EV infrastructure deployment
- Utility companies managing charging demand
- Communities aiming for equitable EV accessibility

---

## Interactive Dashboard
🔗 [View the Tableau Story](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/gowtham.prasad/viz/BIASpring2025Capstone/FinalStory)

The dashboard includes:
- **Charging Station Distribution Map**: Geographic placement of charging stations across Alabama.
- **Accessibility Analysis**: Proximity of stations to population centers and highways.
- **Temporal Trends**: Usage patterns over time (if available).
- **Equity Considerations**: Coverage in rural vs. urban areas.

---

## Data Sources
- Public EV charging station data (federal/state energy datasets, DOE AFDC, etc.).
- Demographic and geographic information from Alabama census and state resources.
- Spatial road network and infrastructure data.

---

## Key Insights
- Identification of **charging deserts** in rural Alabama.
- Correlation between **population density and station placement**.
- Insights into **future infrastructure needs** to support EV adoption.

---

## How to Use
1. Open the Tableau story via the link above.
2. Navigate through interactive dashboards and maps.
3. Use filters to explore:
   - Region-specific coverage
   - Station type (Level 2, DC fast chargers)
   - Accessibility by population and distance.
